Model

A small open economy with domestic frictions and external risk

SONOMA augments a benchmark small open economy with recursive preferences, long-run productivity risk, external debt and equity positions, and domestic corporate financing frictions.

Core mechanism

Persistent credit shocks are priced, corporate leverage shapes domestic dynamics, and domestic financial flows interact with external credit and equity shocks.

Six shocks

State variables that move the economy

1

Short-run productivity

Current productivity growth fluctuations.

2

Long-run growth news

Persistent revisions to expected productivity growth.

3

Domestic credit

Shocks to the tightness of firms' borrowing constraint.

4

External credit

Shocks to the cost of borrowing from abroad.

5

External equity premium

Persistent shocks to expected external equity returns.

6

External equity return

Unexpected realized shocks to net external equity returns.

External balance sheet

The model decomposes net foreign assets into debt and equity components, allowing different adjustment margins and hedging benefits.

Corporate financing

A tax shield encourages debt issuance, while an enforcement constraint limits borrowing and creates a labor-market wedge.
